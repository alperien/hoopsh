# Immersion Patterns in Text-Based Sports Sims (FM, OOTP, EHM/FHM)

Research scout findings, 2026-07-31. For a text/UI basketball franchise sim.
Provenance tags: [P] = verified primary (quoted page text read directly), [S] = secondary (review/summary-level characterization), [I] = inferred (my synthesis).

## 0. Ranked: systems worth stealing vs. avoiding

**Steal (highest leverage first):**
1. Youth intake day as a calendar ritual ("Christmas") — anticipation loop with preview -> reveal -> disappointment/jackpot.
2. Scouting fog of war — perceived vs. true ratings, attribute ranges, scout judgment quality as a stat; uncertainty is the game.
3. The inbox/news feed as the game's spine — the world arrives at you; it is the primary interface, not a side panel.
4. Deep history tracking from day one — almanac, record books, career stats, HoF; the save's past is the content.
5. Emotional attachment machinery — fictional players you discover, develop, and mourn; personalities that shape development.
6. Rhythm/pacing — fast sim loop where "one more game becomes 17 more"; frictionless day-to-day cycle.
7. Small ritual events — deadline day, live draft/cup draws, award nights, milestone stories.

**Avoid / make optional:**
1. Mandatory dialogue-tree press conferences and repetitive player chats (FM's most-hated system).
2. Repetitive template news with irrelevant stats and fake three-comment "social" reactions.
3. Nagging morale micromanagement that reads as noise (OOTP players turn it off).
4. Exploitable/erratic trade AI — the single fastest immersion killer in GM games.
5. Depth hidden behind opaque UI with no feedback (FHM's fatal flaw) or extra clicks (FM26's).

## 1. Football Manager: what creates the pull

### Loved systems

- **Regens/newgens + youth intake day.** Community-wide "Christmas" metaphor is near-universal: "Around youth intake day I always start humming Christmas songs... the tingling feeling of hope that maybe once the rumors of a golden generation will finally prove correct" [P] (https://www.passion4fm.com/youth-intake-guide-how-clubs-produce-newgens/). Also "There's nothing better than youth intake day" [P] (https://fmprojects.substack.com/p/youth-intake), "like Christmas morning" [P] (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cLWa2OL9QvE), and even the disappointment is prized as realistic: "when it arrives you feel disappointed and it always appears that everyone else gets better than you" [P] (https://fmbrotherhood.com/fm-stories/the-kids-arent-all-right-football-manager-2018-story/). Mechanics: a preview email months ahead (rumors of a "golden generation") builds anticipation before the reveal [P].
- **Scouting fog of war.** Star ratings are explicitly *perceived* current/potential ability (PCA/PPA), filtered through the judging ability of your Head of Youth Development / scouts — "Don't read too much into these star ratings... they don't represent the true CA and PA" [P] (passion4fm youth intake guide). Elite play is learning to read through the fog: "attributes aren't the most important... Star ratings may lie" [P] (https://www.passion4fm.com/how-to-scout-wonderkids-newgens-in-football-manager/). Hidden attributes (consistency, big matches, professionalism) surface only via scout report prose [S] (https://www.thehighertempopress.com/2025/09/fm-wunderkinds-2026-scout-filters-hidden-attributes-and-a-5-season-plan/).
- **The email inbox as the game's interface.** "The main interface is really that of emails, which inform you of what needs to be done... This part of the game alone was enough to keep me hooked for almost a hundred hours" [P] (https://thegameenjoyer.substack.com/p/love-found-and-lost-football-manager). FM26 replaced the inbox with a tile portal and reviewers directly blamed it for lost immersion: stories "are hidden behind so much of the difficult UI that it becomes too much of a chore... the loop of the game just becomes moving from match to match without any of the organic world building" [P] (https://www.cubed3.com/games/reviews/pc/football-manager-26).
- **Emergent narrative (the real product).** "Finding a hidden gem who had scored 30 goals in the Bulgarian league and was suddenly being touted as the next big superstar was just one of the many magic stories that naturally emerged" [P] (Cubed3, above). FM framed as the greatest RPG via emergent storytelling [S] (https://loudpoet.com/2023/09/23/is-football-manager-the-greatest-rpg-ever-tldr-yes/). Academic work: players form parasocial bonds with virtual players — "I never saw him play but we were the best of friends for years" [S] (https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/2167479514542152).
- **Player interactions/morale — when consequential.** Loved in the concrete: "You can bring a youth player to the first team... talk to him before the match to hype him up, and he might take it and be eager to impress" [P] (Game Enjoyer, above). Personality-dependent reactions (perfectionists resent unearned praise; overpraise breeds complacency) make people-management a real system [P] (https://fullerfm.com/2023/01/05/player-interactions-in-football-manager/).
- **What FM26's removals revealed as the "jigsaw of immersion":** touchline shouts, social media feed, deadline day, live cup draws, calendar, international management. Cubed3: losing shouts "kills a little bit of that immersion that is central to an experience like this" [P]. AltChar lists deadline day and live cup draws as "painful omissions" [P] (https://www.altchar.com/reviews/football-manager-2026-review-a-disappointing-step-backwards-a6QHj3y0USb4). SI's own studio head conceded: "Immersion is the main element we're focused on" for the next release [P] (https://www.footballmanager.com/news/return-international-management-and-review-fm26).

### Tedious / stale systems

- **Press conferences.** The consensus stale system. "Press conferences – you either hate them or you tolerate them... journalists keep asking the same tired questions"; match reports/news are "often incoherent, poorly constructed, and contain irrelevant statistics" [P] (https://fullerfm.com/2025/05/22/fm-logic-media-press-interactions/). Steam threads: repetitive, boring, want to disable or delegate [S] (https://steamcommunity.com/app/2252570/discussions/0/3943524562547635052/). A dedicated SI forum thread asks "Does anyone actually enjoy the media and player interactions?" [P-title/S-content] (https://community.sports-interactive.com/forums/topic/578796-does-anyone-actually-enjoy-the-media-and-player-interactions/). Trusted Reviews (FM22): "press conferences remain tedious" [S] (https://www.trustedreviews.com/reviews/football-manager-2022).
- **Why they fail [I]:** low variety (same questions), low stakes disguised as high frequency, and answers are a solved min-max (pick the safe option to nudge body language). The delegation trap: you can hand them to an assistant, but "I don't want to risk my assistant saying something stupid" [P] (Fuller FM) — so it's a chore you can't safely skip.
- **Dialogue staleness generally:** "team talks remain stuck with the same generic options... dialogue options that feel like they were written in 2006"; reporters ask the same question about a struggling one-star loanee repeatedly [P] (AltChar, above).

## 2. OOTP: the deepest franchise layer

- **Credential:** "hailed perennially for its depth and its realism... so realistic that multiple MLB clubs" use it; open-ended sandbox with full rule fidelity (service time, arbitration, minor-league options, Rule 5, draft classes, leagues run "into the infinite future") [P/S] (https://www.nytimes.com/athletic/4339875/2023/03/24/out-of-the-park-baseball-24/).
- **Rhythm & attachment (the hook):** "consider the lack of action and gameplay a feature, not a bug. OOTP... lulls you into the rhythm that it requires from you, where one more game becomes 17 more games, and where you end up texting your buddy endless updates about how your rebuild of the Marlins is going" [P] (https://defector.com/playing-a-baseball-simulator-made-me-a-fanatic-again). Attachment to previously unknown players ("Did I know the name Wyatt Langford before I signed him...?") and season-scale narrative memory ("I can tell you all about how my years-long center field battle... has gone") [P] (same).
- **Stat tracking as pedagogy and identity:** robust stats taught a lapsed fan modern sabermetrics and roster rules; realism doubles as a teaching tool [P] (Defector).
- **History/almanac:** archived season data, historical box scores, HTML report snapshots, exports [S] (https://wiki.ootpdevelopments.com/index.php?title=OOTP_Baseball%3AImportant_Game_Concepts%2FTools_Functions_and_Editors%2FThe_Almanac). Fictional-league players run saves with 97–350+ years of history, write player backstories, and say small leagues (8–16 teams) are more immersive because you can know everyone [P] (https://forums.ootpdevelopments.com/showthread.php?t=262389, https://forums.ootpdevelopments.com/showthread.php?t=362161, https://forums.ootpdevelopments.com/showthread.php?t=318293).
- **Personalities:** work ethic/intelligence/leadership shape development and are used as scouting tiebreakers; low values are "major handicaps" [S] (https://forums.ootpdevelopments.com/showthread.php?t=273603, https://forums.ootpdevelopments.com/showthread.php?t=292586).

### What OOTP players skip or fight

- **Storylines (random flavor events):** divisive enough that a prominent forum member campaigned to turn them off in his signature; the dev defense is that they inject real-life randomness and are optional [P] (http://forums.ootpdevelopments.com/showthread.php?p=3559615). Complaints: repeats, era-inappropriate events [S].
- **Morale:** a settings checkbox exists to disable it; threads call clubhouse management intrusive/tedious, with "cancer" players hard to diagnose [S] (https://forums.ootpdevelopments.com/showthread.php?t=224921, https://forums.ootpdevelopments.com/showthread.php?p=4078107). Lesson [I]: mood systems must be legible and actionable or players nuke them.
- **Trade AI (chronic sore spot):** reputation system exploitable — "I have given away players for $5 and I get the same bump in reputation as if I made a blockbuster deal" [P] (https://forums.ootpdevelopments.com/showthread.php?t=345546); "Trading with the AI is basically cheating" [S-title] (https://forums.ootpdevelopments.com/showthread.php?t=352487); AI-to-AI deals gut prospect pools implausibly [S] (https://forums.ootpdevelopments.com/showthread.php?t=345497). OOTP 27 shipped a full deadline-trade-AI overhaul in response [S] (https://www.operationsports.com/out-of-the-park-baseball-27-is-overhauling-trade-ai-to-deliver-more-realistic-deadline-deals/). Mitigations players use: hard mode (no "make this work" feedback), difficulty sliders [S] (https://wiki.ootpdevelopments.com/index.php?title=OOTP_Baseball%3AImportant_Game_Concepts%2FThe_Trading_Model%2FGlobal_and_League_Trading_Options%2FGlobal_and_League_Trading_Options).

## 3. Why text out-immerses graphics

- Academic (Kinephanos, 2016): FM's immersion "almost looks more like that of a board game"; built on number-crunching plus *intertextuality* — pundit comments, interviews, scripted dialogue keyed to real football discourse; the player's imagination does the rendering [P] (http://www.kinephanos.ca/Revue_files/2016_Hocquet.pdf). Crawford's "cult of Champ Man" work: the game works as a conversational/social resource tied to real fandom [S] (https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1080/13691180600858721).
- Defector: no gameplay is "a feature, not a bug"; text pacing mimics the sport's own season rhythm [P].
- **Counter-evidence — graphics can subtract:** FM's 3D engine showing implausible animations ("defenders simply watched the ball roll through") broke one player's trust in the whole simulation and made him quit; commentary/2D never had that problem [P] (https://thegameenjoyer.substack.com/p/love-found-and-lost-football-manager). "Most hardcore FM fans use the classic 2D view anyway... We want functionality, speed, and clarity" [P] (AltChar). Lesson [I]: text lets the sim claim anything plausibly; showing it badly falsifies the claim. A minimal-graphics basketball sim should treat this as permission, not a handicap.

## 4. Generated news: real vs. fake

- **Failure modes (FM):** identical templates ("three comments – one positive, one negative, one neutral" on every story, even signing Yamal for Doncaster on a free); incoherent match reports built on irrelevant stats ("Wolves beat an Everton team that was 3 centimetres taller") [P] (Fuller FM media logic, above). Repetition of the same question/story about marginal players [P] (AltChar).
- **Failure mode (EHM): the news vacuum.** "Sometimes you feel as if you're playing in a vacuum" — team news exists but league-wide news doesn't; AI teams rarely trade, "trade deadline day tends to come and go without a whimper" [P] (https://www.gamespot.com/reviews/nhl-eastside-hockey-manager-review/1900-6104740/). A quiet league reads as dead.
- **What players ask for:** streak/milestone stories that escalate (every 5/10/20 games) and build suspense across updates; news keyed to trades, demands, manager actions, oddball game events [P] (https://forums.ootpdevelopments.com/showthread.php?p=2453806). Editorial voice with multiple variants, rivalry coverage, buildup pieces — explicitly "rather than a raw database dump" [S] (https://github.com/openfootmanager/openfootmanager/issues/205). OOTP players now export save data to LLMs to write recaps/podcasts — proof of unmet demand for narrative framing [S] (https://forums.ootpdevelopments.com/showthread.php?t=365377).
- **Synthesis [I]:** news feels real when it (a) references specific, true save-state facts, (b) has continuity (follows up on its own prior stories), (c) varies voice, and (d) covers the whole league so the world moves without you. It feels fake when reactions are template-random, stats are irrelevant, or the same beat repeats verbatim.

## 5. EHM / FHM: the niche-sport analog

- **EHM did well:** attachment ("close to impossible not to get attached to your team and even to your players once you have traded them out"), "just another game" pull, deep realistic database, multi-level control NHL/AHL/ECHL [P] (https://www.sportsgamersonline.com/news/reviews/eastside-hockey-manager-review/; https://steamcommunity.com/app/301120/discussions/0/4594180031244797017/). Community verdict: it felt "made by real hockey fans," not generic sim devs [P] (Steam thread).
- **EHM did badly:** sparse league-wide news (vacuum), locked database/no editor, overly rigid trade AI ("untouchables" made fair blockbusters impossible) [P] (GameSpot), weak history/stats recording and dated UI [P] (SGO).
- **EHM died of market, not design:** piracy plus a niche sport plus digital-only distribution in 2006; strong reviews could not cover license costs [P] (https://www.eurogamer.net/eastside-hockey-shut-down; https://www.ehmtheblueline.com/site/2007/01/30/si-games-indefinately-halts-development-of-ehm/).
- **FHM's cautionary tale: depth without feedback is worthless.** FHM5: "behind a clunky wall of icons... is one of the most detailed, in-depth, and customizable hockey games on the market," but no tutorial, no visible effect of decisions ("even after hours of fiddling, the picture still looks essentially the same"), slow simming, no jump-to-date, oversimplified trades — 4/10 [P] (https://lwos.life/2018/12/18/franchise-hockey-manager-5-is-the-2018-hockey-gift-that-couldve-been/). AI roster absurdities (No. 1 overall pick never playing in the NHL) instantly destroyed trust: "what's the point if the AI can't even maintain the team properly" [P] (Steam thread).

## 6. Design implications for a basketball text sim [I]

1. Build the draft/intake reveal as the emotional tentpole of the calendar (basketball's draft + lottery is even richer raw material than FM's intake).
2. Scouting uncertainty is a feature: show ranges/grades filtered through scout skill; never expose true ratings.
3. Make the feed the UI. Every system should speak through it; anything the feed doesn't surface effectively doesn't exist (FM26 lesson).
4. News needs continuity, league-wide coverage, escalating streak/milestone beats, and multiple voices; ban irrelevant-stat filler and fixed reaction templates.
5. Trade AI plausibility is the credibility floor — invest there before any flavor system; offer a hard mode with no "make this work" hints.
6. Media/interaction systems: low frequency, high consequence, safely delegable, fully optional. Never a daily dialogue quiz.
7. Track history obsessively (records, award history, retired numbers, almanac) — long saves are the retention engine, and small leagues deepen attachment.
