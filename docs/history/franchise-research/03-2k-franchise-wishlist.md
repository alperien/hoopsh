# NBA 2K Franchise-Mode (MyNBA/MyLeague/MyGM/Eras) — Community Wishlist & Failure Map

Research scout findings, 2026-07-31. Sources: Operation Sports (OS) forums, NLSC, YouTube transcripts (SOFTDRINKTV, KOT4Q, Popboy2K), community press. Tags: [P] = verified primary (forum post / transcript read directly), [S] = secondary (article summarizing community), [I] = inferred by me.

## 1. Biggest complaints, by theme

### 1.1 CPU trade logic (15-year-old complaint, still #1)
- "The CPU trade logic is still broken. Using the 'smart' simulation just means they add protection to picks and pick swaps, but the underlying logic of who is being traded and for what remains mostly illogical." [P] — OS 2K26 MyNBA thread p6: https://forums.operationsports.com/forums/forum/basketball/nba-2k-basketball/26855307-2k26-mynba-franchise-mode/page6
- "Trade logic remains terrible. You either completely fleece the CPU or get fleeced yourself; it's very rare a mutually beneficial trade works out." Also: draft-day CPU trades only fire if you manually watch the whole draft; you can't trade FOR a just-drafted player mid-draft (was possible in old MyLeague); "swap best/worst" pick option removed. [P] — SOFTDRINKTV, "NBA 2K25's MyNBA Mode is BROKEN..." (w/ SimGamingNetwork data): https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aM-58uarAj4
- Complaint is ancient: 2011 OS thread "Is there any way to fix the ridiculous unrealistic CPU trade logic?" [P] https://forums.operationsports.com/forums/forum/basketball/nba-2k-basketball/nba-2k-last-gen/460834-is-there-any-way-to-fix-the-ridicuous-unrealistic-cpu-trade-logic
- Mechanical limits: max 3 trade partners, ~7 asset slots per team — can't recreate real 4-team / 8-asset deals; users beg for a 4th team + more slots. [P] — MyEra Fix Wishlist: https://forums.operationsports.com/forums/forum/basketball/nba-2k-basketball/941073-myera-fix-wishlist
- No sign-and-trade; just-signed players locked 60 days. Second-round picks can only be swap best/worst, not protected. [S/P] — https://www.ssn-sports.com/post/untitled ; MyEra thread above.

### 1.2 Free agency & contracts (most-cited immersion killer)
- CPU declines every team/player option: "Wemby, Paolo, Chet, etc always hit free agency year one" on default settings. Community answer: "Base settings are not optimized... you'll have to find sliders." [P] — https://forums.operationsports.com/forums/forum/basketball/nba-2k-basketball/950984-mynba-teams-always-decline-options
- "Everyone signs one year deals, no team or player options, teams don't retain core players... CPU constantly renounces rights... stars move regularly and on short term deals. Has been for years." [P] — https://forums.operationsports.com/forums/nba-2k-basketball/1015292-free-agency-my-career-broken.html
- Stars too easy to sign (LeBron w/ 14 offers accepts yours); qualifying-offer glitch (unoffered RFAs stay restricted); RFA matching doesn't work (never get chance to match, lose young players for nothing); minimum-tier vets demand $15M; good FAs (Ty Jerome, John Collins, Bobby Portis) sit unsigned all season; productive stars retire way too early (Klay, LeBron, CP3 sim retirements) and unsigned players auto-retire after 1 year; retirement override only lasts one season. [S, detailed primary-style test] — https://www.ssn-sports.com/post/untitled
- OS staff acknowledge "too many good FAs stay unsigned" as a legacy issue with a slider workaround article. [S] — https://www.operationsports.com/nba-2k25-mynba-how-to-avoid-too-many-free-agents-staying-unsigned/
- CPU sometimes makes NO offseason signings at all (30-team-control config bug). [P] — https://forums.operationsports.com/forums/nba-2k-basketball/994900-cpu-not-signing-making-offers-offseason-mynba.html
- CBA depth missing: "no real consequences for hitting the second apron — there should be real penalties like losing trade exceptions and limiting draft flexibility. 2K is the official NBA game; it's time they start acting like it." [S] — ssn-sports above.

### 1.3 Roster/ratings inflation + progression/regression
- 2K25: 21 players 90-99, 120 players 80-89 (2K17 had 63); 140 of 150 starters rated 80+. After 3 sim years: ~25 at 90+, ~150 at 80-89 — "breaks the economic simulation; there isn't enough money in the NBA to pay all these players," and contract sliders can't fix it. [P] — SOFTDRINKTV transcript above.
- CPU auto-signs every draft pick regardless of need → rosters clog, cap burns, QOs forgotten, bird rights waived, "dead cap epidemic," good players waived for rookies. [P] — SOFTDRINKTV.
- Teams stack 5 PGs / ignore holes, hoard mediocre players. [P/S] — SOFTDRINKTV; https://blog.2kdraft.com/post/mynba-problems-and-2k-draft-solutions (note: this blog is marketing a companion app — use as corroboration only).

### 1.4 G-League & two-way contracts
- "Fictional G-League players randomly shoot up to 90 overall... yet if you send a player down for development, morale tanks, ratings drop, and they refuse to re-sign. The entire G-League system is unusable for development." [P] — SOFTDRINKTV.
- Two-way rules wrong for years; user teams forced to carry 15 on NBA roster while CPU can cut to 12 via assignments. "Are the two-way contract rules finally updated?" is a launch-week question every year. [P] — OS 2K26 thread p5: https://forums.operationsports.com/forums/forum/basketball/nba-2k-basketball/26855307-2k26-mynba-franchise-mode/page5
- G-League assignees still pop back into game rotations; hardship exceptions / 10-day contracts effectively broken (users post multi-step manual workarounds to mirror real transactions). [P] — MyEra Fix Wishlist thread above.

### 1.5 Generated draft classes
- Long-running failure (2015→): big men generated with guard sig-skills and vice versa; nonsense tendencies (7-footers with high alley-oop-pass, 2nd-rounders with 80 shoot tendency); absurd wingspans; stamina in the 40s. [P] — https://forums.operationsports.com/forums/forum/basketball/nba-2k-basketball/769078-i-really-hope-generated-draft-classes-are-fixed-next-year
- "Horrible generated draft classes make 2K20 MyGM unplayable" (couldn't edit/import classes in MyGM). [P] — https://forums.operationsports.com/forums/forum/basketball/nba-2k-basketball/881764-horrible-generated-draft-classes-make-nba-2k20-mygm-unplayable
- 2K22 next-gen: ALL generated players had identical tendencies + "very slow release"; US prospects with Eastern-European names; NBA players generated with WNBA animations; class-download bugs. "2K is known for ignoring MyLeague/MyNBA bugs." [P] — https://forums.operationsports.com/forums/forum/basketball/nba-2k-basketball/916945-has-anyone-noticed-the-draft-class-bugs
- Ecosystem response: players rely on hand-made community draft classes; curators rate creators and hand-nerf auto-generated players who out-rate real prospects. [P] — https://forums.operationsports.com/forums/forum/basketball/nba-2k-basketball/nba-2k-basketball-rosters/926486-ps5-draft-classes-in-mynba-eras-mode
- Disagreement: a 2016 thread found generated classes "pretty evenly distributed... busts and surprises" [P] — https://forums.operationsports.com/forums/forum/basketball/nba-2k-basketball/799268-auto-generated-draft-class-quality — minority/older view vs. dominant complaint above.

### 1.6 Injuries
- Recovery times absurd: Lonzo Ball set to max 480 days still returns by All-Star break; prime Rose missed 2 weeks with a broken leg; physio staff silently accelerates recovery; suspensions ride on injury logic so suspended players "recover" early. "MyNBA really needs one cycle that's just clean-up." [P] — https://forumsold.operationsports.com/forums/nba-2k-basketball/1015137-mynba-can-we-please-have-normal-injury-recovery-times.html
- Earlier years: in-game injuries basically never fired for user teams despite sliders; users simmed in a parallel file and manually re-entered injuries. [P] — https://forums.operationsports.com/forums/forum/basketball/nba-2k-basketball/nba-2k-basketball-sliders/906306-no-injuries-during-mynba-season
- 2K26 launch: "I doubt the injuries are fixed but I have a manual way of updating that." [P] — OS 2K26 thread p5.

### 1.7 Sim-stat realism, awards, records
- Patch changed take-shot tendencies → league leaders average ~24 ppg; "2K's roster makers can't be trusted to make rosters that provide accurate MyNBA stats. You always have to adjust tendencies and simulation sliders." [P] — https://forums.operationsports.com/forums/forum/basketball/nba-2k-basketball/952361-mynba-players-stats-in-league-leaders-issue
- "Why is it so hard for them to generate accurate sim stats after 25 years?" Awards overrides are position-locked (guard MVP can only be replaced by a guard); All-Rookie 2nd team displays 2 players. [P] — https://forums.operationsports.com/forums/forum/basketball/nba-2k-basketball/926381-extremely-game-breaking-bug-in-mynba
- No multi-decade historical stats/records browser; users beg for EA-CFB-style year-by-year history, past champions, award history. [S] — ssn-sports above.

### 1.8 QA / trust collapse
- "2K doesn't put effort into any part of the game that doesn't generate microtransaction revenue." [P] — https://forums.operationsports.com/forums/forum/basketball/nba-2k-basketball/939346-nba-2k24-mynba-is-same-as-last-year/page16
- "2K is NOT good for franchise folks anymore. I don't trust them." [P] — OS 2K26 thread p5. Recurrent ask: a "polish-only" year, no new features. [P] — awards-bug thread above.

## 2. MyNBA Eras: what's praised vs. what's shallow
Praised [S/P]:
- "The single coolest feature in sports gaming": 6 starting points (1983/1991/2002/2010/2016/modern), era-accurate broadcast packages, rules, uniforms, short-shorts, sponsors; branding auto-updates as years roll; CBA rule toggles; expansion/contraction control; what-if replays. — https://realsport101.com/article/nba-2k26s-eras-mode-is-the-best-thing-in-sports-gaming-and-no-one-plays-it ; 2K's own feature framing: https://newsroom-uk.2k.com/news/nbar-2k25-showcases-all-new-stephen-curry-mynba-era-and-introduces-mygm-on-playstationr5-xbox-series-xs-and-pc
- Even harsh critics credit it: "the introduction of Eras deserves a lot of credit — that's a TON of work." [P] — OS 2k24 thread p16.

Shallow/half-baked [P/S]:
- NLSC "Duality of MyNBA Eras": "simultaneously comprehensive and half-baked... quantity over quality." Roster completeness varies wildly (community audit by Tropical_Wendigo: Kobe era most complete; Steph era thinnest despite being most recent; Magic/Bird thin); key unlicensed players (Iguodala, Griffin, Wall) missing from the very eras being sold; historical rating/tendency inaccuracies "disrespect NBA history." — https://www.nba-live.com/mto-the-duality-of-mynba-eras/
- Era identity flattens after ~3 seasons (presentation, commentary); draft classes not era-aware (same generation template every decade); MyNBA/MyGM feel like two disconnected modes; save fragility (2K24 delisted, servers cut Jan 1 2026 → long saves need offline durability). [S] — https://www.prismnews.com/hobbies/nba-2k/five-ways-nba-2k-mynba-eras-could-be-improved
- The broken sim layer "ruins the otherwise excellent Eras feature — you can't simulate ahead quickly without these issues arising." [P] — SOFTDRINKTV.

## 3. Sliders/mods community — what it reveals about the vanilla sim
- Schnaidt1's OS slider threads (annual institution, 2K17→2K23): full gameplay slider sets PLUS MyNBA setup guides (rotation setup, offseason handling), and eventually global tendency + badge edits to whole rosters because default ratings/tendencies don't play realistically — and "gameplay changes significantly after year 1" of a save. Sliders only work with his edited rosters. [P] — https://forums.operationsports.com/forums/forum/basketball/nba-2k-basketball/nba-2k-basketball-sliders/927085-schnaidt1-s-nba-2k23-sliders-and-settings
- Popboy2K maintains yearly video sets covering "PROGRESSION, SIMULATION, TRADE, CONTRACT, INJURY, DIFFICULTY, MORALE, CHEMISTRY, BASIC SETUP... every single MyNBA/MyGM slider... to make the whole mode work the way it should" — i.e., every franchise subsystem needs community retuning out of the box. [P] — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IymyVDzgG_M
- Staff badges silently distort gameplay (Tactician boosts 4Q basketball IQ league-wide; Shot Doctor +3 shooting to everyone); MyNBA plays differently from Play Now; realism players disable staff badges and zero the consistency sliders to kill rubber-band "momentum scripting." [P] — https://forums.operationsports.com/forums/forum/basketball/nba-2k-basketball/952113-mynba-cpu-ai-cheese
- Inference [I]: vanilla biases = inflated ratings, over-generous progression, too-short injuries, options-declining contract AI, trade AI without asset valuation, sim engine tuned by tendency tables rather than a possession model. The community's entire modding effort is compensating for missing systemic realism — that's the product gap.

## 4. Immersion features people beg for
- True front-office sim: "realistic salary cap management with meaningful long-term consequences, scouting that rewards genuine research rather than revealing rating bars, coaching staff development with real strategic influence, ownership-level decisions across decades... a true GM experience, not a dressed-up season mode." Franchise players "arguably the most underserved segment," skewing older. [S] — https://www.prismnews.com/hobbies/nba-2k/nba-2k26-features-adult-gamers-want-to-see-added-to-the-game
- Storylines/news/press: MyGM 2K25 relaunch shipped with FOUR conversations total; 2K26 added 30 team-specific scenarios / 98 conversations inspired by real GM situations (rebuild Bulls, Warriors' last Steph run...) — direction welcomed, execution thin; governor doesn't even acknowledge a championship. [P] — KOT4Q: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gatq1B8Wk-4 ; 2K PR: newsroom link above. Broader call for storytelling investment in sports games. [S] — https://realsport101.com/article/more-than-a-roster-update-why-sports-games-must-invest-in-storytelling
- CBA fidelity: aprons w/ real penalties, working QOs/RFA matching, sign-and-trades, hardship exceptions, 10-days, correct active-roster counts, pick protections on 2nds, 4+ team trades. [P/S] — ssn-sports; MyEra Fix Wishlist.
- Expansion done right: expansion-team creation praised, but protection logic absurd (Siakam, Anunoby, Brandon Miller, Markkanen left unprotected across repeated tests). [P/S] — ssn-sports; OS 2K26 thread p6.
- History/legacy: multi-decade stat/award/champion archive; coach carousel realism (fired 25-win coach shouldn't jump to a 1-seed; can't pick assistant coaches anymore — now "bonus staff"); staff contract extensions broken. [S/P] — ssn-sports; 2kdraft blog.
- Presentation texture: context-aware commentary, era/playoff atmosphere; halftime/wrap-around shows. [S] — Prism adult-gamers article.
- Watchable franchise: GM-view CPU vs CPU spectating was requested/added late. [P] — https://forums.operationsports.com/forums/forum/basketball/nba-2k-basketball/948754-nba-2k25-mynba-mygm-franchise-discussion/page4

## 6. Disagreements & caveats
- Depth vs function: near-universal agreement MyNBA is the DEEPEST franchise mode in sports gaming (customization, Eras, expansion) AND that its underlying logic is broken — "pointless depth" (SOFTDRINKTV) vs "customisable for any situation, a lot of issues can be addressed with user inputs" (OS 2k24 thread poster). Both positions are live in the community.
- Draft classes: dominant view = broken/lazy; older 2016 minority view = fine variance (§1.5).
- Eras: "best thing in sports gaming" (realsport101) vs "half-baked, quantity over quality" (NLSC). Not a contradiction — praise is for concept/presentation, criticism for rosters/logic/longevity.
- 2kdraft.com blog corroborates complaints but is selling a companion app; treat its framing as interested. [S]
- Some cited articles (Prism News, shuajota) are aggregation/AI-flavored press; used only where they matched primary forum sentiment. [S]

## 7. Implications for our text-based franchise sim [I]
The community is effectively speccing our product. Ranked by frequency/intensity of demand:
1. Believable trade AI: real asset valuation, pick protections (incl. 2nds), swaps, 4+ team trades, sign-and-trades, draft-night trades, no fleecing in either direction.
2. Full CBA engine: options exercised rationally, working QOs/RFA matching, bird rights respected, aprons with real penalties, trade/hardship exceptions, 10-days, two-ways, correct roster-size rules.
3. Anti-inflation rating economy: stars scarce, league talent distribution stable over 10+ seasons, cap economy that stays solvent.
4. Rational CPU roster building: needs-aware signings, no auto-signing whole draft classes, no 5-PG rosters, sane waiving.
5. Sane free agency: stars hard to sign, market-accurate contract demands, no mass unsigned quality FAs, realistic retirement timing.
6. Era-aware, archetype-coherent generated draft classes (no template tendencies).
7. Realistic injuries: durations tied to injury type, suspensions separate from injury logic, staff effects transparent.
8. Sim stats matching real NBA distributions out of the box — no slider surgery required.
9. Working G-League development loop (assignments help development, no morale death-spiral, no random 90-OVR G-Leaguers).
10. Immersion layer: news/storylines/press around GM decisions, coach carousel realism, multi-decade history/records archive, expansion with sane protection lists.
Meta-lesson: the loudest cross-cutting complaint is that 2K never tests or fixes this layer ("polish, not features"). Reliability of the sim core is itself the killer feature.
