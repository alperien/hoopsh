## MEDIUM packages/career/src/packs.ts:41 - PREP's bonus scheme (one-and-one at 7, double at 10, counted per quarter) matches no NFHS era, and the header claims rule-book truth
The comment (packs.ts:20-21) sells "one-and-one bonus at 7 with double bonus at 10" as "NFHS rules, the shape most states play". Two problems. (a) Era: NFHS eliminated the one-and-one for 2023-24 — the current book awards a flat two free throws at the FIFTH team foul of each quarter, fouls resetting per quarter. (b) Counting scope: the pre-2023 book counted team fouls per HALF (one-and-one at the 7th of the half); the engine counts per period (the NCAA pack works only because its periods ARE halves, rulepack.ts:248-250), so PREP's 7/10 apply per 8-minute QUARTER — a hybrid no rulebook ever printed. Reaching 7 team fouls in an 8-minute quarter is a tail event (NBA-rate ~3.3 fouls per 8 min), so PREP under-awards bonus free throws relative to both real eras; the current rule (5/quarter, flat 2, no one-and-one) is exactly expressible with the existing fields. Same clause: `shotClockOffRebSec: 20` (packs.ts:40) is the NCAA reset; the NFHS 35-second shot-clock guidance resets to the full 35 on an offensive rebound — another invented "rule-book" number.
Breaks: HS free-throw economy for the whole first season of every career; the packs.ts:7-8 rule-truth claim (register C9 covers statistical calibration, not rule truth — the header itself draws that line).
