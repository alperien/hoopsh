## HIGH stock.ts:601 - the combine shock/reprice is unreachable: updateStock always runs before runCombineWeek
`combineThisWeek = stock.combineDone && career.clock.week === career.params.tick.combineWeek` can never be true in the shipped tick. tick.ts:459 runs `resolveWeek` (which calls updateStock at week.ts:278) BEFORE tick.ts:463-465 calls `runCombineWeek` in the same week, and tick.ts:478 increments the week afterwards. So at week 33 updateStock sees combineDone=false, and from week 34 on the week no longer equals combineWeek. Consequences, all confirmed empirically (probe: rank walked 7→1 the week after the combine, move exactly weeklyMoveCap 6, never shockMoveCap 14; the reason string never printed): (a) the combine never arms the shock cap, so the documented one-week reprice (stock.ts:555-557 "shockMoveCap on a shock week: ... the combine"; docs/CAREER.md:287 "measurements go public and reprice you") is smeared over subsequent weeks at 6 picks/week; (b) the reason branch stock.ts:653-656 ("the post-combine reprice lands: up/down N spots") is dead code — the actual reprice week prints the generic board-tighten reason instead; (c) feed hygiene (stock.ts:688-689) treats the real reprice week as a non-shock, so a sub-3-pick post-combine move prints nothing, contradicting the header's "shocks ... always print" (stock.ts:25-29). The only input state that CAN satisfy the flag is combineDone from a previous year plus week==33 of a later year (see the zombie finding below) — a phantom shock a year late. No test covers the combine-week shock path (stock.test.ts calls updateStock before runCombineWeek, same broken order, and never asserts the shock cap or the reprice reason).
Breaks: a documented guarantee (three doc sites) and the pillar-2 legibility of the single most-hyped stock event; the shockMoveCap param is partially dead surface (only games and injuries ever arm it).

## What is done well

- Coverage as a derived pure function (stock.ts:44-46, 340-358) — no stored ledger to drift from its causes across save/load — is a genuinely elegant determinism decision, and the header explains exactly why the shape was chosen.
- The fix-wave headers narrate measured defects with numbers (the noise-walk share of events, the audited ~15-pick mock gap, the chucker inversion) and name their own mirrors and debts inline. This is the honest register the repo demands, applied consistently.
- Feed hygiene is implemented precisely as documented: complete history, curated feed, and the test balances the ledger exactly (first print + every 3-plus move, nothing else).
- The reason chooser (stock.ts:644-682) quotes actual drivers — bracket occasion, TS% over the window, slump length, real tape-measure numbers — so pillar 2 is real code, not aspiration.
- perceiveProspect is a faithful structural mirror of franchise scouting (same 12-slot table layout, same linear ease, same bias-never-shrinks doctrine), with the anchor values cross-referenced to the franchise params they mirror — verified equal (9/4/3).
- Determinism discipline is airtight at the local level: sorted iteration everywhere a Record is walked, fixed draw blocks with the early returns placed after the draws, one-decimal rounding for byte-stable values, unique event ids by construction.
- The production index shares one implementation (and one weight) between stock and recruiting via blendNowRead, so the two consumers cannot price the tape differently — reuse over re-derivation, as the module claims.
- Tests exercise real semantics rather than plumbing: interleaved-read persistence, coverage-MAE monotonicity across 40 prospects, chucker-vs-scorer at identical fog and identical stream ids, capped-walk direction under forked clones, combine idempotency.
