## What is done well

- The noise-floor architecture is the real thing: sampling distributions measured at the gates' exact sample sizes (3×12/24/40 windows per 40-game base, 40 bases), per-statistic sd, and a two-check tripwire — band edge ± 3·sd plus a center-drift check at Zd=3.5 chosen with family-wise false-alarm arithmetic stated in the comment (realism.test.ts:76-79). The z-gate is exactly what CALIBRATION.md claims, applied per stat.
- One algorithm, two adapters, genuinely enforced: the shared row-scan functions (rebMissDeltas, sameSecPairs, subAfterMakeCount, countPossessions) run identically on sim and corpus NeutralRows; corpus reference columns are live-computed at print time, never hand-typed; a byte-identity unit test pins that equivalent sim events and real bbref text render to identical strings.
- The G6 correction is statistically right: at the 180-attempt floor, P(0 makes | 3% heave FG%) ≈ 0.4% — the same ~z=3 convention as the league gates — while rate and decided-share clauses stay armed at any n. The fix matches its CALIBRATION.md description exactly.
- Honest-exclusion doctrine in the measurement path: everything unmappable is dropped symmetrically and COUNTED (NeutralGame.excluded, serialized into scoreboard.json), and the real-side parser aborts loudly above 0.5% unparsed rather than quietly thinning the reference.
- The sweep's objective is carefully engineered: score continuity at band edges (with the reasoning written down), NaN metrics scored +10 rather than skipped so broken wiring can't look like a win, disjoint `-verify` seed suffixes so verification is held-out, rail-pin warnings every run, and a verify-rung exit code.
- Loud-failure discipline is pervasive and incident-cited: checkFlags vocabularies on every CLI, `--games 0` refusal, missing-corpus refusal, the evaluate() NaN-fails-loudly contract for typo'd band metrics.
- aggregate.ts gets the estimator split right and documents why: volume-weighted pooled ratios for fgPct/tpPct/ftPct/astdShare vs mean-of-means for pace/orbPct/ortg, with the two-team-games-per-game accumulation convention spelled out.
