## What is done well
- The floor samples the exact statistic each gate computes: per window it runs the same `sampleMatchup` pairing, the same alternating home/away flip, the same `accumulate`/`finalize` fold (pooled ratios and all), at the same n as the consumer — n24 for the 24-game realism guard, star n12 for the 12-game fidelity gate — so the measured sd really is the sampling sd of the gate's own estimator, not of a proxy.
- Estimator hygiene is right where it counts: sample sd uses n−1 (noisefloor.ts:60); calreport's SE is sd-of-window-means/√windows (the correct SE for a mean of iid window means), never a pooled per-game sd smuggled in; ratio stats are ratios of sums inside each window, with aggregate.test.ts pinning fixtures that would expose the rival convention.
- calreport enforces the third review's lesson in code, not prose: centers strictly from n40 (line 47), gate-σ and se distances reported separately, and an explicit "edge-unresolved" verdict when |d| < 2se — refusing to claim a side of the edge the sample cannot support.
- The two-check realism design (edge tripwire + drift-vs-measured-center) closes the "9σ inside the band = uninstrumented" hole for league metrics, with the multiple-comparisons arithmetic stated and correct (Zd = 3.5, 17 checks → family-wise ≈ 0.79%, honestly rounded to "near 1%").
- Generation-side incident hardening is real: meta derived from the merged DATA rather than the emit invocation's flags (scan b4-7), loud arg parsing via checkFlags/flagNumber so a dangling `--leagueBases` can never silently sample nothing (b4-8/H-03), and defaults pinned to the artifact's 40/16/8 with the halved-power incident documented in place.
- aggregate.ts documents its three divisor conventions (counting per team-game, mean-of-means for pace/orbPct/ortg, pooled for shooting ratios) with basketball reasons, and mergeAcc is honestly labeled UNWIRED with its wiring condition and float-associativity caveat — the doc was corrected rather than the claim defended.
- The regen-in-commit discipline ("the diff IS the drift record") is visible in history: five floor re-baselines, each tied to the landing that moved the streams — which is exactly why the one missing regen (the HIGH) stands out as an outlier rather than a norm.

- "At the gates' sample sizes" holds for every width consumer: realism guard runs 24 games and reads n24 sd; fidelity gate runs 12 and reads star n12 sd; n40 backs calreport centers and the 40-game verify context. CALIBRATION.md's "n12-tier means, n=120 windows" matches gen (n:120 rows). The league n12 tier has no gate consumer (informational only) — checked, not a defect.
